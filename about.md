# Macros Special

Record your inputs on a level and play them back frame-perfectly.

## How to use
1. Open a level and press **pause**.
2. Click the green **macro button** on the left side of the pause menu.
3. Press **Record**. The level restarts and every click is recorded.
   - Works with **practice mode**: when you respawn at a checkpoint, the inputs after it are discarded, so you can grind sections.
4. Pause again, type a name and press **Save**.
5. Press **Load** next to a saved macro and then **Play** to watch it run.

While a macro is playing, your own clicks are ignored and the attempt is treated like a start-pos run: **no progress or completions are saved**.

Macros are stored as `.json` files in the mod's save folder (`geode/mods/saso6000.macros-special/macros`).
